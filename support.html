Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my Premium purchase?

Premium is a one-time (non-consumable) purchase, so you can restore it for free on a new device or after reinstalling — no need to buy it again.

1. Open Pawnheart and go to the Shop.

2. Open the Premium tab.

3. Tap Restore Purchases.

4. Sign in with your Apple ID if prompted. Your Premium content will unlock automatically.

Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase. If restore doesn't find your purchase, contact us at semenetsbeph@gmail.com.

I lost my game progress — can I get it back?

Pawnheart saves your progress locally on your device (there are no online accounts or cloud saves). This means:

  • Deleting the app deletes your save file.
  • Progress does not automatically transfer between devices.
  • Your Premium purchase, however, is tied to your Apple ID and can always be restored (see above).

If your progress disappeared unexpectedly while the app was still installed, please email us with details about what happened and we'll try to help.

How do I contact the developer?

Email semenetsbeph@gmail.com with your question, feedback, or bug report. Please include your device model, iOS version, and a description of the issue.

Does Pawnheart collect my data?

No. Pawnheart has no ads, no analytics, and no tracking, and it does not collect personal data. All game data stays on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.
